The society

Alpha is student-run and open to the whole campus, led mostly by students from LAF and adjacent finance programs. It teaches technical finance to people who are not yet professionals — so the system is precise and plain: rules, grids and flush-left labels, with no decoration standing in for rigor.

Logo

Locked

The letters Alph set in Kollektif, completed by a calligraphic α that is fixed artwork — never typed, never substituted by a font character.

White on navy is the primary form; navy on the light ground is the secondary. Single color only: no gradient, outline, second color or shadow inside the lockup.

Typography

Compose larger gaps as multiples of 8. Radius is 0 everywhere, on purpose.

Grid and rules

Equal-width cells and 2 px dividers between major sections. Don't soften a rule into a hairline or replace it with whitespace. Lay sibling groups out with flex or grid plus gap.

07

Imagery

Documentary photography of real events and real members: workshops, company visits, panels, podcast recordings. Every content photograph goes through the .grayscale wrapper — pure black and white, no tint, no duotone, no rounded corners, no shadow.

Caption sits below, flush left

Charts follow the same logic: ink lines on the ground, one accent series, no gradient fills, no 3D. Label series directly instead of with a legend where it fits.

08

Iconography

Lucide throughout, 1.5–2 px stroke, no fill, ink by default and accent only when the icon carries the action. Sizes 16, 20 or 24 px, optically aligned to the cap height of adjacent text. Never mix another family, never use emoji.

Take every color, font, space, radius and shadow from the variables. A value that isn't in the tokens is a decision this document hasn't made yet — add it here before shipping it.
